# How to play GTX

GTX is a browser open-world game in a Vice City mood. Run `npm install` then `npm run dev`, open the local URL, and click the canvas to lock the mouse.

**On foot:** WASD or arrows move; mouse looks; wheel zooms. Shift sprints, Space jumps, left click attacks, right click aims.

**Vehicles:** Press E next to a car to drive (F for passenger). WASD throttle and steer, S brakes, Space handbrake-drifts, R changes the radio. E again to exit.

**Goal:** Explore the city, steal cars, pick up cash and weapons, and follow mission markers. Crimes raise wanted stars; lose the cops before you get busted.

**Menus:** Tab toggles the minimap. Escape pauses. Use the pause menu to resume, save, or load.
